## Overview

<>
  Fanavera was developed as a classified listings and discovery platform centered
  on user-generated content, with a strong emphasis on backend scalability and
  moderation controls.
</>

## Key Features

<ul>
  <li><strong>Relational Content Model:</strong> Structured relationships between users, listings, and categories.</li>
  <li><strong>Moderation State Machines:</strong> Controlled content lifecycle from submission to approval.</li>
  <li><strong>Ethical Data Ingestion:</strong> Safeguards for responsible handling of user-generated content.</li>
  <li><strong>SEO-Aligned Metadata:</strong> Search-friendly URLs and metadata generation.</li>
  <li><strong>Administrative Dashboard:</strong> Monitoring of listings, moderation activity, and system health.</li>
</ul>

## Technologies Used

<ul>
  <li><strong>PHP and SQL:</strong> Backend architecture and relational modeling.</li>
  <li><strong>HTML, CSS, JavaScript:</strong> Frontend presentation and interaction.</li>
</ul>

## Challenges and Learnings

<>
  Building a user-generated platform highlighted the complexity of moderation
  workflows and data accountability.
</>

## Outcome

<>
  Fanavera operates as a scalable and structured marketplace platform with strong
  moderation controls and improved discoverability.
</>
