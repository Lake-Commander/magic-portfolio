## Overview

Fanavera is a digital marketplace designed to aggregate and present third-party products and services in a clean and trustworthy environment. The platform focuses on usability, searchability, and ethical content sourcing.

## Key Features

- **Category-Based Marketplace Structure**: Organized listings for efficient discovery.
- **Affiliate and Third-Party Integration**: Supports external product sources without direct inventory handling.
- **Advanced Search and Filtering**: Improved content discovery across categories.
- **User Trust Features**: Emphasis on transparency and listing quality.

## Technologies Used

- **PHP and SQL**: Marketplace logic and data storage.
- **JavaScript**: Dynamic filtering and interactions.
- **Custom Plugin Architecture**: Extended platform features without core modification.
- **SEO Optimization**: Structured listings for better visibility.

## Challenges and Learnings

Designing a marketplace without direct inventory required clear boundaries between platform responsibility and third-party content. This reinforced ethical design and system clarity.

## Outcome

Fanavera now serves as a growing digital marketplace with a scalable architecture and clear content governance.
