## Overview

This project explores transactional retail data to identify revenue drivers, branch performance differences, and customer behavior patterns that influence operational and marketing decisions.

## Key Features

- **Branch Comparison**: Revenue and gross income by branch.
- **Customer Behavior**: Payment method and rating patterns.
- **Trend Exploration**: Time-based patterns that can inform staffing and promotions.

## Technologies Used

- **Python (pandas)**
- **Visualization**: matplotlib

## Challenges and Learnings

Retail datasets often hide key insights behind segmentation. The project emphasizes slicing by branch, product line, and payment type to surface real drivers.

## Outcome

A clean EDA case study demonstrating business-first analysis and strong visualization storytelling.
