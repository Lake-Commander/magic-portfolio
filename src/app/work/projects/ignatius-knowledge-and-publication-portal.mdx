## Overview

This platform supports faculty-level academic communication, departmental content, and official publications within an institutional environment. The focus was on system stability, structured content management, and maintainability.

## Key Features

- **Structured Content Management**: Consistent schemas across departments.
- **CMS Customization**: Minimal plugin dependency to reduce fragility.
- **Backend Stability**: Reliable deployment and content workflows.
- **Institutional Publishing Support**: Clear separation of official and informational content.

## Technologies Used

- **PHP and SQL**: Backend processing and data storage.
- **HTML and CSS**: Clean presentation for institutional content.
- **CMS Configuration**: Tailored administrative workflows.

## Challenges and Learnings

Maintaining flexibility while ensuring long-term stability reinforced the importance of disciplined CMS customization and schema consistency.

## Outcome

The faculty portal provides a stable and organized platform for academic communication with reduced maintenance overhead.
