## Overview

<>
  This platform was built to support faculty-level academic communication,
  departmental content, and official publications. The focus was on backend
  stability, structured content management, and long-term maintainability.
</>

## Key Features

<ul>
  <li><strong>Structured Content Schemas:</strong> Consistent data models across departments and publications.</li>
  <li><strong>CMS Customization:</strong> Minimal plugin dependency to reduce system fragility.</li>
  <li><strong>Backend Stability Controls:</strong> Reliable deployment and content workflows.</li>
  <li><strong>Institutional Publishing Support:</strong> Clear separation of official and informational content.</li>
</ul>

## Technologies Used

<ul>
  <li><strong>PHP and SQL:</strong> Backend processing and data storage.</li>
  <li><strong>HTML and CSS:</strong> Clean presentation for institutional content.</li>
</ul>

## Challenges and Learnings

<>
  Balancing flexibility with stability highlighted the risks of excessive CMS
  customization and reinforced disciplined configuration practices.
</>

## Outcome

<>
  The faculty portal now provides a stable and structured platform for academic
  communication with reduced maintenance overhead.
</>
