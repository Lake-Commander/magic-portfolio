## Overview

IJEE is a peer-reviewed academic journal platform designed to support structured research publishing with a strong emphasis on content integrity and long-term archival access.

## Key Features

- **Structured Article Publishing Pipeline**: Controlled ingestion and release of academic manuscripts.
- **CMS-Backed Editorial Governance**: Centralized management of journal content.
- **SEO-Aware Content Rendering**: Search-friendly URLs and metadata.
- **Archive Optimization**: Efficient access to historical publications.

## Technologies Used

- **PHP and SQL**: Backend workflows and content storage.
- **HTML and CSS**: Readable layouts for academic material.
- **JavaScript**: Navigation and browsing enhancements.
- **Query Optimization**: Indexed queries for archival performance.

## Challenges and Learnings

Handling growing volumes of scholarly content highlighted the importance of performance tuning and database normalization in read-heavy systems.

## Outcome

IJEE now runs on a reliable platform that supports consistent publication cycles and improved research visibility.
