## Overview

<>
  The IJEE platform was engineered to support peer-reviewed academic publishing with
  an emphasis on structured content management, discoverability, and long-term
  archival access. The system was designed to preserve editorial integrity while
  remaining performant as content volume increased.
</>

## Key Features

<ul>
  <li><strong>Structured Publishing Pipeline:</strong> Controlled article ingestion and publication workflows.</li>
  <li><strong>CMS-Backed Governance:</strong> Centralized editorial management with enforced schema consistency.</li>
  <li><strong>SEO-Aware Rendering:</strong> Clean URLs and metadata generation for improved indexing.</li>
  <li><strong>Archive Performance Tuning:</strong> Optimized retrieval of historical publications.</li>
</ul>

## Technologies Used

<ul>
  <li><strong>PHP and SQL:</strong> Backend workflows and content storage.</li>
  <li><strong>HTML and CSS:</strong> Semantic layouts optimized for academic reading.</li>
  <li><strong>JavaScript:</strong> Navigation and interaction enhancements.</li>
</ul>

## Challenges and Learnings

<>
  Managing a growing scholarly archive emphasized the importance of database
  normalization and performance tuning in read-heavy systems.
</>

## Outcome

<>
  IJEE now runs on a maintainable and efficient platform that supports consistent
  publication cycles and reliable access to archived research.
</>
